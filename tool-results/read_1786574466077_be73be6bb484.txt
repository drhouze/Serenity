  1140→- **Root cause**: The `filterSnapshotToFacilities` function in `src/lib/backup-filter.ts` accepted a `PrismaClient` parameter and ran DELETE statements against `main."table"` — but the caller (`/api/backup`) passed a fresh `new PrismaClient()` that connected to the LIVE database (via DATABASE_URL env var).
